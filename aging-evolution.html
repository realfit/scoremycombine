The mission

What can you still do?

Aging Evolution inspires adults to rediscover their athletic potential. REALFIT provides rigorous, repeatable measurement that turns that inspiration into verified accomplishment. Together, this event answers the question every former athlete has asked: how much is still there?

The competition principle: In each challenge, the winner is the oldest verified athlete who meets or exceeds the fixed threshold — not the athlete with the highest raw performance. Age, established to the day, is what matters once the standard is met. If no one meets the threshold, no winner is declared.

The headline search

How old is too old?

Five provocative questions. Who is the oldest person who can still serve 120 mph? Throw a football 50 yards? Pitch 80 mph? Run a 5.0-second 40? Drive a golf ball 300 yards? The oldest verified qualifier in each challenge becomes a central subject of the Aging Evolution documentary.

The participant experience

Find out where you stand.

You don't have to hit the headline standard to make the event matter. Every participant earns a REALFIT®-certified result, a ranking in their five-year age group, and a verified benchmark to build on. "You don't have to be the best. You just have to show up."

What certification means

Your result is official.

Tier 2 Measurement

Every test is administered by a REALFIT®-certified official using specified equipment and published protocol. Your result meets the standard required for official recognition.

FITDAT® Recorded

Your result is recorded in the open FITDAT® format — a structured, versioned data record tied to your athlete account. Comparable across any REALFIT®-powered event, now or in the future.

Age-Group Ranking

Every participant receives a performance ranking in their five-year age bracket — regardless of whether they meet the headline threshold. Your certified result stands on its own.

Shareable Result

You'll receive a verified result document carrying the REALFIT® certification mark — something worth sharing, because it means something.
