About the event

Prove that your best isn't behind you.

The Aging Evolution Combine is the first event of its kind — a structured, standardized athletic challenge built specifically for competitors over 40, powered by REALFIT® certified scoring.

Aging Evolution believes that the most powerful thing an athlete over 40 can do is measure themselves against an objective standard — not against their younger self, not against peers, but against a defined benchmark that proves capability.

This is not a recreational event. Every measurement is taken by a certified official using precise REALFIT® protocols. Your result means something.

Across five disciplines — tennis, football, speed, golf, and baseball — participants compete for a single REALFIT® Certified score that reflects real athletic output.

Results are scored against REALFIT® Reference Standards: population-calibrated benchmarks that tell you exactly where your performance falls and what it represents.

Your result carries the "Certified by REALFIT®" mark — a verifiable record of what you did on that day, measured the same way for everyone.

What certification means

Your result is official.

Tier 2 Measurement

Every test is administered by a REALFIT®-certified official using specified equipment and protocol. Your result meets the standard required for official recognition.

FITDAT® Recorded

Your result is stored in the open FITDAT® format — a structured, versioned data record you own. Compare against any REALFIT®-powered combine, now or in the future.

Reference Band Score

Your composite score is placed on a calibrated reference scale. You'll know your number and what it means — how you compare to the defined standard for each challenge.

Shareable Result

You'll receive a verified result document carrying the REALFIT® certification mark and your Aging Evolution Combine scores — something worth sharing, because it means something.
