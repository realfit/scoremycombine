The one requirement

Follow the FITDAT® standard.

REALFIT® is an open platform. Any organization — a school, a league, a gym, a military branch, a sports federation, a media company — can build a combine using our infrastructure. There is no approval process for the type of tests you run, the population you serve, or whether participants tackle the full combine or a single test.

The only requirement: every test and scoring methodology must follow the FITDAT® specification. Tests must be precisely defined. Measurements must be reproducible. Standards must be openly documented. This is what makes a REALFIT®-powered result meaningful — and what earns the certified mark.

How it works

From idea to published combine.

1

Define your population & purpose

Tell us who you're testing, what you want to measure, and what a great result looks like for your community.

2

We design your combine

REALFIT® selects and specifies tests from the FITDAT® library, defines how individual and composite scores are calculated, and establishes reference standards for your population.

3

Set up your branded platform

Your score set gets a custom URL, your branding, and a live scoreboard. Participants interact with your program.

4

Train your officials

REALFIT® certifies the individuals running assessments so every measurement meets the Tier 2 standard required for official results.

5

Measure & publish results

Participants complete assessments. Scores are recorded in FITDAT® format, posted to the scoreboard, and each participant receives their certified result.
